# Better Water (Dusklight mod)

A native `.dusk` mod that draws a real, animated water surface over the stock water in
Twilight Princess: waves, refraction, screen-space reflections, depth tint and shoreline foam.
Small or shallow bodies of water (puddles, pots) are left alone so they stay stagnant.

Built on the Dusklight mod template; Dusklight itself is not modified.
See `res/water.wgsl` for the shader and `src/mod.cpp` for water detection.
